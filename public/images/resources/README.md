# EVADA Resources images

Place real resource card thumbnails, video poster images and documentation preview images here.

Update the matching entry in `src/data/resources.ts`:

- `thumbnailSrc` for article or document thumbnails
- `posterSrc` for video and webinar poster images
- `imageAlt` for accessible image descriptions

Keep `assetStatus` as `coming-soon` until the image and destination are real.
